# QCGS problem map after literature reopening

**As of:** 2026-10-08

**Stage:** P1 source-backed map; the candidate question is an empirical extension, and novelty is not established. See [reference audit](literature-verification.md) and [gap reassessment](literature-gap-reassessment.md).

## Setting and present evidence

Ramen runs one fixed CLIP ViT-B/16 test-time update over class-partitioned gradient memory. Within one query's retrieved pool it can legally replace one support gradient with another from the same pseudo-class while preserving cardinality. Existing reports say the support pool has oracle CE headroom, the tested single-view entropy-sign ranking stopped, and the tested multi-view target-θ_R ranking stopped in Stage A. Raw records are not in this checkout, so those remain prior reported/validated outcomes rather than a new recomputation.

The open question is a local counterfactual: with the query, memory snapshot, action space, Ramen update and evaluator fixed, which label-free observation—if any—orders same-query CE utility across legal support substitutions? Ranking exact objective change and ranking an inexpensive estimate are different claims; first-order estimator work remains downstream of exact objective evidence.

## Position and exit

The papers answer adjacent but different questions: how to adapt CLIP, how to retrieve/query memory, how to choose whole TTA procedures across streams, and when broad action choice is identifiable. The current candidate gap is narrower: whether a small, prespecified set of exact label-free objective changes ranks **individual support substitutions** inside one fixed Ramen query/cache/update, measured against the same query's evaluator-only CE. This may be a useful diagnostic/extension; the reviewed literature does not establish its novelty or practical value.

The case priorities are qualitative and conditional:

| Candidate | Importance / headroom | Observability and feasibility | Decisive test / disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1, exact local objective information | High decision value for deciding whether to stop estimator engineering: whole-method identifiability and per-query selective TTA are prior work, while Ramen's local objective alignment is unmeasured. | Exact objective changes and evaluator-only CE after one fixed action are computable for each query. Stage A feasibility depends on action batching and 8 GiB memory. | Freeze three distinct source-backed objectives and measure exact score-to-utility relation over every legal same-pseudo-class swap. Contribution/novelty remains provisional; do not claim general identifiability. |
| G2, contrastive/source-aligned evidence | High mechanism relevance for CLIP because pretraining is contrastive; a gain in another TTA task does not establish support-swap alignment. | Frozen text embeddings and current-batch pseudo-caption probabilities are available without labels; the contrastive score has higher batch-level compute/state requirements. | Measure a CLIPTTA-style soft image-to-text contrastive loss after the same Ramen action, with a frozen pseudo-caption rule and current batch only. |
| G3, reliability/abstention | Practical value remains because harmful replacements and baseline-correct/wrong heterogeneity are reported, but the general adapt/skip problem and CAS signal are prior work. | Entropy, fixed-view disagreement, CAS, and cache consensus are observable; baseline correctness and true OOD stay evaluator-only. Any fitted gate is supervised development and must be disclosed. | Only after L0/L1 if a meaningful fixed action/policy remains: test CAS and a predeclared no-swap rule on independent IDs against always-adapt/always-abstain and matched random, with risk–coverage and harm. Do not claim selective-adaptation novelty. |
| G4, neighborhood/memory evidence | Moderate-to-high for Ramen because support candidates and cached predictions are its mechanism; retrieval alone is already well represented in prior VLM-TTA. | Current cache snapshots and per-support provenance are available. A score that mutates or reads future cache state invalidates parity. | Conditional enrichment: one query-local support-consensus signal, with cache/update held fixed and an explicit stream-history control. |
| G5, estimator geometry | Low immediate value until an exact objective has usable information; gradient matching and data attribution already have a large supervised literature. | A first-order approximation can reuse cached gradients but cannot rescue an objective whose exact delta does not track CE utility. | Defer until one exact objective passes the independent information gate; then compare estimate-to-exact fidelity separately from exact-to-utility alignment. |

G1 is the first bounded experiment, G2 provides one source-aligned candidate within it, G3/G4 are conditional alternatives, and G5 is downstream. This order separates information from reliability and approximation rather than creating a new set of heuristic mixtures.

P1 can advance to P2 as a bounded diagnostic, not a new generic action-identifiability or selective-adaptation claim. The decisive test remains the exact per-action objective audit. A no-signal result closes only those formulations in this Ramen state and leaves broader evidence-channel claims open.
